## Context

`research-workbench-runtime-dashboard-chart-v1` loads the market's last 30 days of candles through the shared `marketResourceCache`, with no polling and no Workbench coupling. The Workbench already has an EMA overlay endpoint (Research `GET /api/market/indicators/ema`, Engine-computed, with warm-up of 5 periods before the window and a cached full history for 200/500/1000) and an episode layer (`features/episodes/`: `EpisodeLayersPrimitive`, `useEpisodeHistories`, `EpisodeToolbar`, Engine route `POST /v1/ema-stack-episodes/history` through `POST /api/market/ema-stack-episodes/history`).

## Decisions

- **D1 Per-strategy configuration from Runtime.** The panel calls `GET /runtime-api/v1/strategy-instances/{id}/chart-layers` once per selection of a strategy (and again on Refresh). The answer carries `ema_stack` (role, timeframe, period) and `episodes` (ref, effective params). The frontend does not parse the specification and does not resolve defaults. Failure of this one call leaves candles and trade layers as they are and shows one line "Chart layers unavailable" with Retry.
- **D2 EMA lines = Research endpoint, no frontend math.** Runtime's `ema_stack` entries carry `timeframe` exactly as Engine's plan states it: the literal `"base"` (the strategy's base timeframe, which is the chart's) or a concrete timeframe such as `"1h"`. For each entry with `timeframe` `"base"` or equal to the chart timeframe, and `source` `close` (the Research endpoint computes EMA of the close): `GET /api/market/indicators/ema?symbol&timeframe&period&from&to_open_time_ms` for the candle window. The same warm-up reasoning as in the Workbench applies and needs nothing here: Research asks Engine for warm-up before `from`. Lines are stored per (symbol, timeframe, period) for the session as half-open ranges like candles; only a missing tail is requested after Refresh. At most three line series exist, created once with the chart; selecting another strategy changes their data, colours by role (fast, anchor, slow), and hides unused ones.
- **D3 Other timeframes and sources.** A stack EMA whose timeframe is a concrete one different from the chart's, or whose source is not `close`, is not drawn and no request is sent; the panel shows "EMA {tf} {period} is not drawn (other timeframe or source)". Not solved here: aligning a higher-timeframe EMA to the base chart.
- **D4 Episodes whole, screen clips.** The history is loaded by `useEpisodeHistories` unchanged: every page newest first, pinned to page 1's `market_data_hash`, restart on `409 market_data_version_changed`, session cache per (ticker, timeframe, params, side). The episode primitive draws what intersects the visible window; an episode that starts before the 30 days is cut at the left edge and its touch numbers stay the Engine's. Nothing is recomputed. Parameters: `historyParamsFromEffective(params)` of the chosen ref; with several refs the toolbar asks for an explicit choice (as the Workbench does); with none, no episode layer.
- **D4a History whole, display strictly inside the window, panning loads nothing.** Three separate things: (1) the episode history is loaded whole, as in the Workbench, because an episode may start before the window; (2) what is drawn is strictly inside the 30-day candle window: the episode primitive gets only the window's candle times and draws no episode element outside them, and EMA lines have points only inside the window; (3) every number of a touch, wave and false break is the Engine's, nothing is recomputed. Panning or zooming the chart never extends the candle window and never starts a candle, EMA or episode request; the Runtime Dashboard is an observation window, not the Research Workbench. The window moves only with the right edge on Refresh.
- **D5 No polling.** The Workbench refreshes `current` after every candle on a timer. Here it is not wired: `current` is refreshed together with candles and lines on Refresh (page 1 without a pin, `refreshCurrent`). No timers.
- **D6 Strategy switch inside a market.** Candles and the cached histories stay; only the layer configuration is read again (D1) and lines or episodes of parameters not yet loaded are requested. Selecting a strategy of another market aborts the previous requests like candles do.
- **D7 Series count stays fixed.** Candles, one projection primitive, one rings series, one cycles series, one open-trade series (existing) plus three EMA line series and one episode primitive. No series is added or removed after the chart is created. "Native viewport only" is widened to say so; the ban on viewport control and on Workbench imports stays. `features/episodes/` is allowed (no Workbench context in it); its primitive gets one optional flag, `clipToCandles`, so that panning left never draws an episode outside the 30-day candle window.
- **D9 Partial results.** Candles and trade layers do not depend on the layer configuration, EMA lines or episodes. State is kept per piece: the configuration (per selected strategy), each EMA line (per symbol, timeframe, period), each side's episode history (per ticker, timeframe, parameters, side). Each has `loading`, `ready` and `error`; an error shows a mark and a Retry for that piece only. Rendered layers are looked up by the selected `strategy_instance_id` and its configuration, so nothing of another strategy is visible after a switch. Retry repeats only pieces not `ready`.
- **D8 Out of scope.** Episode inspector table, touches table, window/break override inputs, EMA values on hover, ATR/RSI overlays, higher-timeframe EMA alignment, episodes for a strategy without the section (no fallback to the stack), any new Engine or Research route.

## Risks

- First load of the full episode history is 12–14 s on BTC 5m (measured on the Mac, Workbench smoke); the panel shows "Loading episodes…" while candles and lines are already drawn.
- Three more series and the primitive redraw on pan; the Workbench draws the same primitive over a larger window, so no new cost class.
